My Unofficial Local Guide to Hamilton Heights

  1. Riverbank park

    2. Riverbank Park in Hamilton Heights — A True Gem Riverbank State Park in Hamilton Heights is nothing short of amazing — a 28-acre, multilevel recreational paradise perched 69 feet above the Hudson River . This unique urban oasis, built atop the North River Wastewater Treatment Plant, offers an extraordinary mix of athletic, cultural, and family‑friendly amenities, from an Olympic‑size indoor pool and seasonal skating rink to tennis courts, a running track, playgrounds, and even a whimsical carousel.

  2. Alexander Hamilton's home

    3. Alexander Hamilton’s only home, Hamilton Grange National Memorial, is a historic Federal‑style house in St. Nicholas Park, Hamilton Heights, Manhattan, and is now a U.S. National Memorial operated by the National Park Service. The neighborhood itself is named after Hamilton, who lived here in the last two years of his life

  3. Dominican food

    4. Dominican food is a staple in Hamilton Heights, reflecting the neighborhood's large Latinx community and its proximity to Washington Heights. Locals and visitors frequently stop by spots like Caridad Restaurant on Broadway for rotisserie chicken, yaroa, and mofongo, Malecón Express Restaurant for roasted chicken and mofongo, Brisa Dominicana for stews and rice and beans, La Flor De Broadway for homestyle platters, and Empanadas Monumental for $3.90 empanadas. With abundant takeout options, family meals, and hearty classics like plantains and roasted meats, Dominican cuisine remains one of the most popular and accessible dining choices in the area.
